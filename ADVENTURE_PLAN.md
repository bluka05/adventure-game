# Road to the World Cup

## Game Overview

You are the star player of your country's national soccer team. The FIFA World Cup has arrived, and every decision you make could change the tournament.

## Story Start

The World Cup is about to begin. Fans are cheering as your Home team prepares for the opening match.

### Choices

- Practice
- Rest

## Decision Point 2

If you choose Practice:

### Choices

- Shoot
- Pass

If you choose Rest:

### Choices

- Study Opponent
- Relax
